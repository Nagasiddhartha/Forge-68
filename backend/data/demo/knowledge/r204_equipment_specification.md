# SPEC-EQ-R204: Equipment Engineering Specification for Reactor R-204

## Document Metadata
- Document ID: SPEC-EQ-R204
- Classification: INTERNAL
- Equipment: R-204, P-201, E-301
- Version: 2.1
- Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM): Heavy Vessel Engineering Ltd.

## 1. Technical Data & Vessel Dimensions
- Vessel Tag: R-204
- Service: Second-Stage Hydrocracker Fixed-Bed Catalytic Reactor
- Inside Diameter: 3,200 mm
- Tangent-to-Tangent Height: 24,500 mm
- Total Internal Volume: 48.5 m³
- Total Dry Weight: 285 metric tons
- Catalyst Operating Weight: 62 metric tons (dense-loaded)

## 2. Design Pressures & Temperatures
- Internal Design Pressure: 38.0 bar gauge
- Operating Pressure: 31.2 bar gauge
- External Vacuum Design: Full vacuum at 150°C
- Design Temperature: 460°C
- Minimum Design Metal Temperature (MDMT): -29°C at 38 bar

## 3. Metallurgy and Fabrication
- Base Metal Material: SA-336 Grade F22V (2.25Cr-1Mo-0.25V normalized and tempered alloy steel)
- Internal Cladding: 4.5 mm nominal thickness weld overlay of Type 347 stainless steel
- Gasket Specification: Spiral-wound 316SS with flexible graphite filler on ASME B16.5 Class 2500 flanges
- Associated Unit Interconnects:
  - Upstream Feed Booster Pump: P-201 (centrifugal 4-stage pump)
  - Reactor Effluent Exchanger: E-301 (shell and tube exchanger, 2.25Cr tubes)
