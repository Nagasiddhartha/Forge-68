# IR-2025-088: Non-Destructive Ultrasonic Inspection Report for Reactor R-204

## Document Metadata
- Document ID: IR-2025-088
- Classification: CONFIDENTIAL
- Equipment: R-204
- Inspection Date: 2025-05-14
- Lead Inspector: Lead NDT Level III Specialist

## 1. Executive Summary
Non-destructive examination (NDE) utilizing Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT) and Time-of-Flight Diffraction (TOFD) was conducted on the pressure shell, longitudinal welds, and circumferential weld seams of Hydrocracker Reactor R-204 during the scheduled spring shutdown.

## 2. Shell Thickness Measurements
- Nominal Design Shell Thickness: 75.0 mm (including 4.5 mm corrosion allowance)
- Measured Shell Thickness (Ring 1 Top Head): 74.6 mm
- Measured Shell Thickness (Ring 2 Middle Course): 73.9 mm
- Measured Shell Thickness (Ring 3 Bottom Course): 73.2 mm
- Minimum Recorded Local Thickness: 72.8 mm observed at the nozzle neck of N-2 (effluent nozzle).
- Analysis: All observed thicknesses remain comfortably above the minimum required structural retirement thickness of 68.2 mm. Current estimated corrosion rate is 0.04 mm/year.

## 3. Weld Seam Examination Findings
- Circumferential Seams C-1, C-2, and C-3: Zero planar defects or cracks detected.
- Longitudinal Seam L-1: Minor isolated slag inclusion noted at depth 34 mm, dimension 2.1 mm length. Judged acceptable per ASME Section VIII Division 2 standards.
- Cladding Integrity: Stainless steel 347 weld overlay showed no signs of hydrogen-induced disbonding or blister formation.
