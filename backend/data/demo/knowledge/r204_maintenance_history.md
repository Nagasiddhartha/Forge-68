# MH-R204: Historical Maintenance & Turnaround Log for Reactor R-204

## Document Metadata
- Document ID: MH-R204-LOG
- Classification: INTERNAL
- Equipment: R-204
- Maintenance Unit: Static Mechanical & Reliability Section

## 1. Turnaround Event — October 2024
- Catalyst Bed Maintenance: Depleted top catalyst skim conducted. Removed top 15% of bed (approximately 9.3 metric tons) and reloaded with fresh Criterion NiMo extrudates.
- Internal Distributor Trays: Cleaned coke deposits from vapor-liquid distribution tray. Re-torqued tray support clips to 45 Nm.
- Bottom Manway: Disassembled bottom 24-inch manway. Replaced damaged spiral-wound gasket with new 316SS/graphite gasket. Flange face passed visual and liquid penetrant inspection.
- Relief Valve Overhaul: Dual safety relief valves RV-204A and RV-204B were removed, cleaned, pop-tested at 35.0 bar, and recertified.

## 2. Unscheduled Intervention — June 2023
- Flange Leak Resolution: Minor hydrogen weeping identified on thermocouple nozzle TE-204-03 during operational survey. Hot-bolting performed per procedure ENG-77 under nitrogen blanket; weeping ceased.
- Instrumentation Calibration: Differential pressure transmitters dPT-204-01 and dPT-204-02 were calibrated across 0-5.0 bar range.
