# SOP-R204: Standard Operating Procedure for Hydrocracker Reactor R-204

## Document Metadata
- Document ID: SOP-R204-REV4
- Classification: INTERNAL
- Equipment: R-204, P-201, E-301
- Version: 4.2
- Facility: Synthetic Refinery Unit 2

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the normal operating parameters, startup sequencing, and steady-state monitoring protocols for Hydrocracker Reactor R-204.

## 2. Operating Limits & Thresholds
- Normal Operating Pressure: 31.2 bar gauge
- Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP): 35.0 bar gauge
- High Pressure Alarm: 33.5 bar gauge
- High Pressure Emergency Trip: 35.0 bar gauge
- Normal Operating Temperature: 395°C to 415°C
- Maximum Bed Temperature: 440°C
- Temperature Ramp Rate: Maximum allowable rate of heating or cooling is 25°C per hour to prevent thermal shock to the vessel cladding.

## 3. Catalyst Bed Differential Temperature Monitoring
Operators must monitor the temperature rise across catalyst Bed 1 and Bed 2 at 15-minute intervals. If the differential temperature (Delta-T) across Bed 1 exceeds 38°C, increase hydrogen quench flow via valve QCV-204A immediately.

## 4. Normal Startup Sequence
1. Verify nitrogen purge is complete with oxygen concentration below 0.1% volume.
2. Align effluent flow path through heat exchanger E-301.
3. Start feed booster pump P-201 on minimum recirculation flow.
4. Establish hydrogen circulation loop and pressurize R-204 to 15.0 bar prior to firing the feed preheater.
5. Ramp preheater at 20°C/hour until reactor inlet reaches 320°C.
