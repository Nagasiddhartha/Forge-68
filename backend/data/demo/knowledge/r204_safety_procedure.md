# SAF-R204: Emergency Depressurization and Safety Protocols for Reactor R-204

## Document Metadata
- Document ID: SAF-R204-EP
- Classification: RESTRICTED
- Equipment: R-204
- Safety Integrity Level: SIL-3
- Effective Date: 2025-01-01

## 1. Safety Interlocks & Emergency Depressurization (EDP)
Reactor R-204 is equipped with an automated emergency depressurization system designed to protect against runaway exotherms and catastrophic vessel rupture.

### Automatic Activation Triggers
- Reactor pressure exceeds 35.0 bar gauge for > 3 consecutive seconds.
- Any bed thermocouple temperature exceeds 445°C.
- Manual trip initiated via control room ESD push button PB-204.

### Depressurization Sequence
1. Immediate trip signal sent to feed booster pump P-201 and preheater fuel gas emergency shutoff valves.
2. High-speed blowdown valve BDV-204 opens to the high-pressure flare header within 2.0 seconds.
3. System target depressurization rate: 2.0 bar/minute until vessel internal pressure reaches 7.0 bar.
4. Hydrogen feed valves XV-204-H2 close automatically.
5. High-pressure nitrogen purge valve XV-204-N2 opens to maintain inert vessel blanket.

## 2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) & Isolation Boundaries
- Any personnel entering the R-204 reactor deck during operational runs must wear Level B protective equipment with personal hydrogen sulfide (H2S) monitors.
- Lock-Out Tag-Out (LOTO): Maintenance requiring reactor vessel entry mandates blind flange installation on nozzles N-1, N-2, and quench lines with double block and bleed isolation verified by operations.
